About ACNHS

Armenian College of Nursing & Health Sciences

Institutional Overview

The Armenian College of Nursing & Health Sciences (ACNHS) is an institution of higher education in Armenia dedicated to the preparation of competent, ethical, and practice-ready nursing professionals. The College is committed to academic quality, clinical rigor, professional integrity, and the advancement of nursing and health sciences education through structured, student-centered instruction.

ACNHS seeks to prepare graduates who demonstrate sound clinical judgment, evidence-based practice, professional responsibility, and readiness for service in diverse healthcare environments. The institution's academic model emphasizes strong theoretical preparation, supervised skills development, and a disciplined foundation for lifelong professional growth.

Mission

The mission of the Armenian College of Nursing & Health Sciences is to educate and prepare competent, ethical, and practice-ready nurses who are equipped to provide safe, evidence-based, and compassionate care across diverse healthcare settings. The College is committed to delivering high-quality nursing education grounded in scientific knowledge, clinical excellence, and professional standards that support professional development and lifelong learning.

Vision

The Armenian College of Nursing & Health Sciences aspires to be a leading institution in nursing and health sciences education, recognized for academic rigor, clinical excellence, and international relevance. The College seeks to advance the profession by preparing graduates who demonstrate clinical competence, critical thinking, integrity, and meaningful readiness to contribute to healthcare systems in Armenia and beyond.

Values

ACNHS is guided by the following core institutional values:

Excellence

We uphold high standards in teaching, learning, scholarship, and clinical preparation.

Professionalism

We promote responsibility, accountability, ethical conduct, and respect in all academic and professional settings.

Patient Safety

We recognize safety, quality of care, and evidence-based practice as foundational principles of nursing education.

Clinical Competence

We value the development of sound clinical judgment, practical skill, and disciplined professional readiness.

Integrity

We promote honesty, transparency, and ethical decision-making in institutional life and professional formation.

Lifelong Learning

We encourage intellectual curiosity, reflection, and continuous growth throughout the professional journey.

Global Perspective

We prepare students to engage thoughtfully with diverse healthcare environments, cultures, and systems.

Compassion

We affirm the importance of empathy, dignity, and patient-centered care in all healthcare practice.

Collaboration

We value teamwork, communication, and interdisciplinary cooperation as essential to effective healthcare delivery.

Academic Governance & Institutional Standards

ACNHS operates as an Armenian institution of higher education within the legal and academic framework of the Republic of Armenia. The College maintains institutional policies, academic oversight, and internal quality standards designed to support educational integrity, professional formation, and responsible governance.

The institution is committed to maintaining clear academic expectations, structured administrative processes, and standards-based educational delivery. Academic governance at ACNHS is intended to support student learning, faculty responsibility, institutional accountability, and the orderly administration of academic affairs.

Regulatory Disclaimer

Licensure eligibility is determined by the applicable nursing or professional regulatory authority in the jurisdiction where a graduate seeks recognition or licensure. Completion of an academic program does not itself guarantee licensure, employment authorization, credential acceptance, or professional registration in any particular country or region.

Graduates may be required to satisfy additional conditions established by the relevant authority, which may include credential evaluation, examinations, language proficiency, supervised practice, background review, and other jurisdiction-specific requirements.
