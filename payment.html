Application Filing Fee Policy

1. Nature of the Fee

The Application Filing Fee (hereinafter “the Fee”) in the amount of one hundred United States dollars (USD $100.00), plus an applicable service charge of five percent (5%) totalling one hundred and five United States dollars (USD $105.00), is a mandatory, one-time administrative fee levied by the Armenian College of Nursing & Health Sciences (hereinafter “the College” or “ACNHS”) upon submission of an application for admission to any programme of study offered by the College.

2. Purpose and Allocation of the Fee

The Fee is collected exclusively to defray administrative and governmental costs arising from the processing and evaluation of the applicant’s submitted documents. These costs include, but are not limited to: (a) document authentication, apostille verification, and notarial review fees; (b) translation and legalisation charges required for documents not originally issued in Armenian or English; (c) fees levied by, or payable to, the Ministry of Education, Science, Culture and Sport of the Republic of Armenia (hereinafter “the Ministry”) in connection with the official assessment, equivalency determination, and recognition of foreign or domestic academic credentials; (d) internal administrative processing costs incurred by the Admissions Office of the College; and (e) any applicable regulatory or compliance costs mandated by Armenian law or Ministry regulation in force at the time of application.

3. Non-Refundability

The Fee is strictly and unconditionally non-refundable in all circumstances, including but not limited to: (a) voluntary withdrawal of the application by the applicant at any stage prior to or following receipt of an admission decision; (b) the College’s decision to reject, defer, or conditionally accept the applicant; (c) the applicant’s failure to satisfy academic, documentary, or procedural admission requirements; (d) force majeure events, including but not limited to natural disasters, armed conflict, pandemic, or governmental order; and (e) any technical, financial, or personal circumstances cited by the applicant after payment has been successfully processed.

The non-refundable nature of the Fee reflects the irrecoverable nature of third-party costs incurred upon submission of an application, including fees already remitted to or reserved for the Ministry and other governmental bodies at the time of application.

4. Payment Processing

Payment is processed through Stripe, a PCI DSS Level 1–certified payment service provider. The College does not store, retain, or have access to the applicant’s card number, CVV, or banking credentials. The 5% service charge represents the cost of payment processing and financial transaction infrastructure and does not constitute a separate governmental or academic fee.

5. Acknowledgement and Consent

By checking the acknowledgement box and submitting payment, the applicant irrevocably confirms that: (a) they have read, understood, and agree to be bound by this Application Filing Fee Policy in its entirety; (b) they consent to the collection and processing of the Fee for the purposes described herein; (c) they waive any right to request a chargeback, reversal, or refund of the Fee except in the event of demonstrable fraudulent or unauthorised processing; and (d) they acknowledge that submission of payment does not guarantee an offer of admission to the College.

6. Governing Law

This Policy is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of Armenia. Any dispute arising from or in connection with the Fee shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts of the Republic of Armenia.

7. Contact

For questions regarding this Policy, please contact the ACNHS Admissions Office at admissions@acnhs.am or visit acnhs.am.
