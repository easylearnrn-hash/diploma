Application Filing Fee Policy

1. Nature of the Fee

The Application Filing Fee (hereinafter “the Fee”) in the amount of one hundred United States dollars (USD $100.00), plus an applicable service charge of five percent (5%) totalling one hundred and five United States dollars (USD $105.00), is a mandatory, one-time administrative fee levied by the Armenian College of Nursing & Health Sciences (hereinafter “the College” or “ACNHS”) upon submission of an application for admission to any programme of study offered by the College.

2. Purpose and Allocation of the Fee

The Fee is collected exclusively to defray administrative and governmental costs arising from the processing and evaluation of the applicant’s submitted documents. These costs include, but are not limited to: (a) document authentication, apostille verification, and notarial review fees; (b) translation and legalisation charges; (c) fees levied by the Ministry of Education, Science, Culture and Sport of the Republic of Armenia in connection with the assessment and recognition of academic credentials; (d) internal administrative processing costs; and (e) any applicable regulatory or compliance costs mandated by Armenian law.

3. Non-Refundability

The Fee is strictly and unconditionally non-refundable in all circumstances, including but not limited to: (a) voluntary withdrawal of the application at any stage; (b) the College’s decision to reject, defer, or conditionally accept the applicant; (c) the applicant’s failure to satisfy academic, documentary, or procedural requirements; (d) force majeure events; and (e) any technical, financial, or personal circumstances cited after payment has been successfully processed.

4. Payment Processing

Payment is processed through Stripe, a PCI DSS Level 1–certified payment service provider. The College does not store, retain, or have access to the applicant’s card number, CVV, or banking credentials. The 5% service charge represents the cost of payment processing infrastructure.

5. Acknowledgement and Consent

By checking the acknowledgement box and submitting payment, the applicant irrevocably confirms that: (a) they have read, understood, and agree to be bound by this Policy; (b) they waive any right to request a chargeback or refund except in the event of demonstrable fraudulent processing; and (c) they acknowledge that submission of payment does not guarantee an offer of admission.

6. Governing Law

This Policy is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of Armenia. Any dispute shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts of the Republic of Armenia.

7. Contact

For questions, contact the ACNHS Admissions Office at admissions@acnhs.am or visit acnhs.am.
