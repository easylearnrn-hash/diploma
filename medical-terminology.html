The core of a word — almost always a body part

Medication Suffixes & Drug Classes

Nursing 101 Pharmacology — recognize any drug by its suffix
Pharmacology

Every drug suffix is a shortcut to the drug class. Know the suffix → know the action, uses, and what to monitor. This is high-yield for NCLEX.

SUFFIX EXPLORER
-pril
ROOT
angio/tens-
Vessel tension
SUFFIX
-pril
ACE inhibitor class
SYSTEM
Cardiovascular
ACE Inhibitors
Blocks Angiotensin I → II conversion → vasodilation. Used for hypertension, heart failure, and diabetic nephropathy.
Used For
  • Hypertension
  • Heart failure
  • Diabetic nephropathy
Monitor
  • BP & Potassium (hyperkalemia)
  • Renal function (BUN/Cr)
  • Dry cough
  • Angioedema
Common Examples
Lisinopril (Prinivil/Zestril) Enalapril Captopril
Side Effects (common)
  • Dizziness / hypotension
  • Dry cough
Adverse / Complications
  • Angioedema
  • Hyperkalemia
Key Cautions / Interactions
  • Pregnancy contraindicated
  • Avoid K+ supplements / K-sparing meds unless directed

X · Core Medication Classifications

Every drug belongs to a class. Knowing the class tells you the action, uses, and what to monitor — without memorizing individual drugs.

🩺
GI · Pain · Fever · Inflammation
  • Anti-emetics
    Prevent or relieve nausea and vomiting
  • Antipyretics
    Reduce fever
  • Anti-inflammatories
    Reduce inflammation
  • NSAIDs
    Reduce pain, inflammation, and fever (Non-Steroidal)
  • Corticosteroids
    Suppress inflammation and immune response
  • Antacids
    Reduce stomach acid (e.g. Calcium carbonate)
  • Antidiarrheals
    Reduce GI motility
  • Laxatives
    Promote bowel movement
  • Antispasmodics
    Relieve smooth muscle spasms
🫀
Cardiovascular · Blood
  • Antihypertensives
    Lower blood pressure
  • Antiarrhythmics
    Regulate abnormal heart rhythms
  • Antilipemics
    Lower cholesterol levels
  • Anticoagulants
    Prevent blood clot formation
  • Antiplatelets
    Prevent platelet aggregation (reduce clot formation)
  • Thrombolytics
    Break down existing blood clots
  • Vasodilators
    Relax blood vessels
  • Vasopressors
    Increase blood pressure
  • Diuretics
    Remove excess fluid via kidneys
⚗️
Endocrine · Metabolic
  • Hypoglycemics
    Lower blood glucose
  • Hyperglycemics
    Increase blood glucose
  • Thyroid Hormones
    Replace or supplement thyroid function
  • Antithyroid Agents
    Reduce thyroid hormone production
  • Antianemics
    Increase RBC production (e.g. Iron, EPO)
🫁
Respiratory · ENT
  • Bronchodilators
    Open and widen airways
  • Antitussives
    Suppress cough
  • Expectorants
    Loosen mucus
  • Mucolytics
    Break down mucus
  • Decongestants
    Reduce nasal congestion
  • Antihistamines
    Block histamine (H1) receptors
🧬
Immune · Infectious Disease
  • Anti-infectives
    Treat bacterial, viral, fungal infections
  • Antiretrovirals
    Treat HIV
  • Antineoplastics
    Treat cancer (Chemotherapy agents)
  • Immunosuppressants
    Suppress immune system activity
  • Immunostimulants
    Stimulate immune response
  • Vaccines (Immunizations)
    Provide acquired immunity
