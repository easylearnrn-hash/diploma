Tuition & Payment Disclaimer

All tuition payments made to ARNOMA / ACNHS are final and non-refundable.

By submitting payment, the student acknowledges and agrees that no refunds, credits, or reversals will be issued under any circumstances, including but not limited to the following:

  • Failure of exams, assessments, quizzes, simulations, or clinical requirements
  • Academic probation, dismissal, suspension, or withdrawal
  • Voluntary discontinuation or decision not to continue the program
  • Absence from classes, simulations, or clinical sessions
  • Personal, medical, family, financial, or immigration-related reasons
  • Schedule changes, time conflicts, or inability to attend classes
  • Transfer requests or non-completion of the program
  • Delays in progress, repetition of coursework, or extended time to graduate
  • Denial of licensure, certification, or examination eligibility by any authority

All payments are applied toward educational services, instructional resources, faculty time, administrative costs, and program infrastructure, and are therefore non-refundable once processed.

Submission of payment constitutes full acceptance of this policy. This policy applies regardless of payment method, date of payment, or amount paid.
The non-refundable payment disclaimer above will be included on the printed invoice.

Payment Terms & Schedule

  • The enrollment (down) payment may be split and paid in multiple partial installments, provided the full enrollment amount is completed as agreed.
  • Monthly tuition payments must be made on the first day of each month.
  • Each monthly payment is considered due between the 1st and the 5th day of the month.

Late Payment Policy:

  • If a monthly payment is not received by the 5th day of the month, a late fee of 0.5% per day will be applied starting on the 6th day and will continue to accrue daily until the balance is paid in full.

Example:

  • February tuition payment is due February 1–5.
  • Beginning February 6, a 0.5% daily late fee will be added until payment is received.

Failure to comply with the payment schedule may result in restricted access to academic materials, records, or services, in accordance with institutional policy.
