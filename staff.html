Faculty Qualifications Statement

The Armenian College of Nursing & Health Sciences maintains a faculty staffing standard consistent with national and international nursing education requirements. All faculty members assigned to nursing courses hold a minimum of a Bachelor of Science in Nursing (B.S.N.) or equivalent degree, with graduate-level qualifications required for senior instructional roles. Faculty responsible for foundational science instruction hold appropriate terminal or advanced degrees in their respective disciplines.

Clinical faculty and simulation coordinators maintain current licensure and professional certification relevant to their areas of instructional responsibility. The College conducts ongoing faculty development and maintains documentation of all faculty credentials in accordance with regulatory and accreditation standards. Full faculty credential files are maintained by the Office of the President and are available for institutional review upon formal request.
