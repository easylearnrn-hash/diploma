Academic Advising

Academic Advising & Counseling

Every student is assigned a faculty advisor for the duration of the program. Your advisor is your primary point of contact for academic planning, course concerns, and progression questions.

What Your Advisor Can Help With

  • Understanding your academic standing and GPA
  • Course selection and progression planning
  • Academic probation and improvement plans
  • Navigating program policies and procedures
  • Preparing for NCLEX and graduation requirements
  • Referrals to tutoring, counseling, or financial aid

Schedule an advising appointment through the Student Portal or email the advising office. Advisors hold open office hours weekly — check the academic calendar for times.

Tutoring

Tutoring & Academic Support

Free tutoring is available to all enrolled students. Peer tutors and faculty-led review sessions cover all core nursing courses.

Available Support Services

📚
Peer Tutoring

One-on-one sessions with trained student tutors. Available by appointment through the Academic Support Center.

🎓
Faculty Review Sessions

Small-group exam review sessions facilitated by course faculty. Scheduled before major exams — see course syllabi for dates.

💉
Skills Lab Open Hours

Practice any clinical skill at your own pace during open lab hours. Skills lab staff are available to assist and coach.

🧠
NCLEX Prep Resources

Access to NCLEX-style question banks, ATI modules, and test-taking strategy workshops available through the college library and LMS.

✏️
Writing Center

Support for nursing care plans, scholarly papers, and APA formatting. Available in-person and via email review.

Wellness

Mental Health & Wellness Support

Nursing education is demanding. We recognize that student wellness is inseparable from academic success. ACNHS provides confidential mental health and wellness resources for all students.

Counseling Services

  • Confidential individual counseling sessions
  • Stress management and coping skills workshops
  • Peer support groups for nursing students
  • Crisis support and referral services

Self-Care Resources

  • Mindfulness and meditation resources available on the student portal
  • Wellness newsletter with tips for nursing students
  • Study break lounge and quiet spaces on campus

Seeking support is a sign of strength, not weakness. All counseling services are completely confidential and will not affect your academic standing.

Crisis Resources

If you or someone you know is in crisis:

  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: Call or text 988
  • Crisis Text Line: Text HOME to 741741
  • Campus Emergency: Contact campus security or call 911
Financial Aid

Financial Aid & Scholarships

The Financial Aid Office assists students with tuition planning, payment plans, and scholarship applications.

Available Financial Resources

  • Institutional Scholarships: Merit-based and need-based awards for qualifying students
  • Tuition Payment Plans: Flexible payment schedules available — contact the financial aid office to enroll
  • External Scholarship Database: Curated list of nursing-specific scholarships updated each semester
  • Emergency Financial Assistance: Limited emergency funds available for students facing unexpected financial hardship

Scholarship applications open at the beginning of each academic year. Check the student portal for current opportunities and deadlines.

Accessibility

Accessibility & Accommodations

ACNHS is committed to providing equal access to education for all students. If you have a documented disability or condition that affects your academic performance, you may be eligible for accommodations.

Types of Accommodations

  • Extended time on examinations
  • Quiet testing environment
  • Alternative formats for course materials
  • Assistive technology support
  • Note-taking assistance

How to Request Accommodations

  1. Contact the Disability Services office or speak with your academic advisor
  2. Submit documentation from a licensed healthcare provider
  3. Meet with the disability services coordinator to discuss your needs
  4. Receive your official accommodation letter and share it with your instructors

Accommodation requests should be submitted before the start of the semester whenever possible. Late requests may not be honored for past assessments.
