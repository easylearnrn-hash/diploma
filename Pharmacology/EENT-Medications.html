🧠 NCLEX KEY: Atropine is CONTRAINDICATED in glaucoma — it increases IOP by closing the drainage angle. Timolol eye drops can cause systemic bradycardia.

👂 Ear (Otic) Medications

Amoxicillin

  • First-line for otitis media (ear infection)
  • 10-day course in children under 2

Ototoxic Drugs — AVOID

  • Gentamicin (aminoglycoside)
  • Furosemide (loop diuretic)
  • Aspirin (high doses)
  • SE: hearing loss, tinnitus

Meclizine / Ondansetron

  • Vertigo → Meclizine (Antivert)
  • Nausea from vertigo → Ondansetron

🧠 NCLEX KEY: Oxymetazoline (Afrin) max 3 days — longer use causes rebound nasal congestion. Pseudoephedrine raises BP.

👄 Throat & Upper Airway Medications

Strep Throat

  • Penicillin or Amoxicillin — first-line
  • Azithromycin — if penicillin allergic

Oral Thrush (Candida)

  • Nystatin — "swish and swallow" or "swish and spit"
  • Fluconazole — systemic/severe cases
  • Common in patients on antibiotics or inhaled steroids

Croup (Emergency)

  • Dexamethasone — reduce airway swelling
  • Racemic epinephrine (nebulized) — severe croup
  • Watch for rebound swelling after epinephrine

Epiglottitis (Emergency)

  • IV Cefotaxime or Ceftriaxone
  • Dexamethasone to reduce swelling
  • Do NOT examine throat — may close airway

⚡ NCLEX Must-Know

  • Atropine CONTRAINDICATED in glaucoma (↑ IOP)
  • Timolol eye drops → systemic bradycardia via absorption
  • Ototoxic meds: gentamicin, furosemide, aspirin (high dose)
  • Oxymetazoline (Afrin) = max 3 days (rebound congestion)
  • Nystatin for oral thrush: swish and swallow/spit
  • Croup: dexamethasone + racemic epinephrine
