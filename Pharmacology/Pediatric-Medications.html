👶 Pediatric Medications

Neonatal, infant, and pediatric pharmacology — weight-based dosing and special considerations

🍼 Newborn Mandatory Medications

Vitamin K (Phytonadione)

  • IM injection at birth
  • Prevents hemorrhagic disease of the newborn
  • Newborns have low Vitamin K (gut bacteria not established)

Erythromycin Eye Ointment

  • Given within 1 hour of birth
  • Prevents neonatal conjunctivitis (gonorrhea, chlamydia from birth canal)
  • May blur vision temporarily (expected)
🧠 NCLEX KEY: All newborns receive Vitamin K (IM) + erythromycin eye ointment. Parents may refuse — educate on risks.

🩸 Diabetes / Blood Sugar

Insulin

  • Regular, Lispro (rapid), NPH (intermediate), Glargine (long)
  • Type 1 diabetes, DKA
  • SE: hypoglycemia

Emergency Hypoglycemia

  • Glucagon IM/SQ → severe hypoglycemia (unable to swallow)
  • D50 IV → emergency hypoglycemia
  • Oral glucose → if conscious and can swallow

❤️ Cardiac Medications

  • Digoxin — ↑ contractility (heart failure, SVT); check apical pulse 1 min before giving; hold if <90 bpm in infants; monitor K⁺; toxic range
  • Furosemide (Lasix) — fluid overload in CHD; monitor K⁺; SE: ototoxicity
  • Captopril — ACE inhibitor for congenital heart disease; ↓ afterload
  • Propranolol — SVT, tetralogy of Fallot spells; ↓ HR
🧠 NCLEX KEY: Digoxin in infants — always take apical pulse for full minute before giving. Hold if HR <90 bpm in infants (vs. <60 in adults).

🧠 Seizure & ADHD Medications

Acute Seizures

  • Lorazepam/Diazepam — status epilepticus (IV/rectal)
  • Ensure airway; position on side; O₂ ready

Seizure Prevention

  • Phenytoin, Levetiracetam (Keppra), Valproic acid
  • Monitor levels; teratogenic potential

ADHD

  • Methylphenidate (Ritalin), Adderall
  • SE: ↓ appetite, insomnia, ↑ HR
  • Give in morning; monitor growth

🌬️ Respiratory

  • Albuterol — asthma rescue (nebulizer or MDI)
  • Racemic epinephrine — croup emergency (watch for rebound swelling)
  • Palivizumab (Synagis) — RSV prevention in high-risk infants; IM monthly during RSV season (Nov–March)

🍽️ Gastrointestinal

Constipation/GI

  • Polyethylene glycol (MiraLax) — constipation
  • Omeprazole — GERD

Cystic Fibrosis

  • Pancrelipase (Creon) — enzyme replacement
  • Take with EVERY meal/snack
  • Do NOT chew (mouth sores)

🩹 Skin Medications

  • Zinc oxide — diaper rash prevention/treatment
  • Nystatin cream — yeast/candidal diaper rash
  • Permethrin 1% — lice (Nix)
  • Permethrin 5% — scabies (Elimite)

⚡ NCLEX Must-Know

  • Vitamin K IM at birth → prevents hemorrhagic disease
  • Erythromycin eye ointment → prevents gonorrhea/chlamydia conjunctivitis
  • Digoxin: apical pulse 1 min; hold if <90 bpm in infant
  • Pancrelipase: take with meals; never chew
  • Palivizumab: RSV prevention in high-risk infants, monthly IM
  • All pediatric doses: mg/kg × weight; check safe dose range
