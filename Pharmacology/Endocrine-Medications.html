🦋 Thyroid Medications

Hypothyroidism — Levothyroxine (Synthroid)

  • Take in morning on empty stomach
  • Don't take with calcium, iron (interfere with absorption)
  • Monitor TSH, T3/T4, HR, weight
  • Don't switch brands (bioavailability differs)
  • SE: palpitations, insomnia, weight loss if overdosed

Hyperthyroidism

  • PTU / Methimazole — block thyroid hormone synthesis
  • SE: agranulocytosis — report sore throat/fever immediately
  • PTU preferred in first trimester (Methimazole teratogenic)
  • Radioactive Iodine (I-131) — destroys thyroid tissue
  • SE: radiation precautions; avoid pregnancy/children for days
🧠 NCLEX KEY: Levothyroxine = morning, empty stomach. PTU/Methimazole = monitor CBC (agranulocytosis). Report sore throat immediately.

💊 Adrenal Medications

  • Prednisone / Hydrocortisone — Addison's disease, inflammation, autoimmune
  • SE: ↑ blood glucose, weight gain, infection risk, osteoporosis, mood changes, fluid retention
  • NEVER stop abruptly — taper slowly (adrenal suppression)
  • Fludrocortisone — aldosterone replacement in Addison's
  • SE: fluid retention, hypokalemia, hypertension

🦴 Bone & Parathyroid Medications

Osteoporosis

  • Alendronate (Fosamax) — bisphosphonate
  • Take with full glass of water
  • Sit/stand upright 30 min after (esophageal irritation)
  • Calcium + Vitamin D supplements
  • Calcitonin — alternative for osteoporosis; also ↓ pain

Parathyroid

  • Cinacalcet — hyperparathyroidism
  • Calcium gluconate/Vitamin D — hypocalcemia
  • IV fluids + Calcitonin — hypercalcemia crisis

⚡ NCLEX Must-Know

  • Insulin peak times = hypoglycemia risk window — assess patient then
  • Clear before cloudy when mixing insulin; Glargine NEVER mixed
  • Metformin: hold before IV contrast (renal/lactic acidosis risk)
  • Levothyroxine: morning, empty stomach, no Ca/iron
  • PTU/Methimazole: agranulocytosis — report sore throat
  • Prednisone: never stop abruptly (adrenal crisis)
  • Alendronate: upright 30 min after to prevent esophagitis
