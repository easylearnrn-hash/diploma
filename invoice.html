Payment Instructions

Payment Terms & Schedule

  • The enrollment (down) payment may be split and paid in multiple partial installments, provided the full enrollment amount is completed as agreed.
  • Monthly tuition payments must be made on the first day of each month.
  • Each monthly payment is considered due between the 1st and the 5th day of the month.

Late Payment Policy:

  • If a monthly payment is not received by the 5th day of the month, a late fee of 0.5% per day will be applied starting on the 6th day and will continue to accrue daily until the balance is paid in full.

Example:

  • February tuition payment is due February 1–5.
  • Beginning February 6, a 0.5% daily late fee will be added until payment is received.

Failure to comply with the payment schedule may result in restricted access to academic materials, records, or services, in accordance with institutional policy.
