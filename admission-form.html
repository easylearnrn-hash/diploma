Transfer Documents Required

Required for Credit Transfer Evaluation: All documents must be official and sealed by the issuing institution. Unofficial transcripts may be used for initial review but official documents will be required for final credit transfer approval.

Additional Information

📋 Transfer Credit Evaluation Process:
1. Submit all required transfer documents
2. ACNHS will review your transcripts and course content
3. You'll receive a transfer credit evaluation within 2-3 weeks
4. Approved credits will be applied to your program requirements
5. Note: Only courses with a grade of C or higher may be transferred
