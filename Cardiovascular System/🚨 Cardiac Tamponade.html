Cardiac Tamponade  
Treatment  
Condition/Cause  
Monitor  
Cardiac tamponade is the accumulation of fluid  
around the heart, often due to pericarditis with  
bacteria-containing fluid or chest injury, which  
increases pressure on the heart and impairs its  
function, with the pericardial space normally  
containing lubricating fluid for smooth heart  
movement.  
1. Pericardiocentesis:  
1. EKG strip with one QRS complex up  
and one down for monitoring.  
● A procedure to remove excess fluid from the  
pericardial sac using a needle.  
2. Any fluid aspiration can lead to  
hypotension; ensure the patient is  
well-hydrated before the procedure.  
● Has both therapeutic and diagnostic effects. The  
fluid is cultured to identify bacterial or microbial  
presence and antibiotic sensitivity, guiding  
appropriate treatment.  
Patient Assessment  
2. Fluid Resuscitation:  
Questions to ask the patient:  
Symptoms  
● Intravenous fluids to maintain blood pressure and  
cardiac output.  
● Does the pain radiate?  
● Is the pain above 8 on the pain  
scale?  
● Does your cough produce  
blood-tinged sputum?  
Sharp Chest Pain: Pain during expiration or  
coughing.  
● Hydrate the patient before any fluid aspiration to  
prevent hypotension.  
Orthopnea: Difficulty breathing when lying  
flat; a supine position is not recommended.  
SOB: Difficulty breathing even at rest or with  
minimal exertion.  
3. Treatment of Underlying Cause:  
● Address the root cause, such as infection, cancer,  
or autoimmune disease.  
Note: Myocardial infarction (MI) does  
not typically present with a cough.  
4. Emergency Action:  
Beck’s Triad (Hallmark Symptoms):  
● Hypotension  
● Since cardiac tamponade is life-threatening, call a  
physician immediately.  
PULSUS PARADOXUS  
● Jugular Venous Distension (JVD)  
● Muffled or Diminished Heart Tones  
Pulsus Paradoxus: An exaggerated decrease  
in systolic blood pressure during inspiration,  
where the systolic pressure drops significantly  
while diastolic pressure remains the same,  
causing the pressures to get closer to each  
other.  
● Cardiac tamponade requires immediate medical  
attention to prevent severe complications or death.  
Seek emergency medical care if suspected.
