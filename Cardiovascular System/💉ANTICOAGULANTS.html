 TRANSITIONING FROM HEPARIN  
TO WARFARIN (NCLEX TIP)  
When a patient is hospitalized and receiving Heparin, and the plan is to  
discharge them on Warfarin, you must overlap both medications for several  
days.  
易 WHY?  
Heparin = Rapid onset (works immediately)  
Warfarin = Slow onset (takes several days to a week to become therapeutic)  
⚠ NCLEX RULE:  
Never stop Heparin until Warfarin is therapeutic!  
✅ WHAT TO DO:  
Start Warfarin while the patient is still on Heparin (usually 5–7 days before  
discharge)  
Continue both medications until the INR reaches 2–3  
Once INR is therapeutic, stop Heparin  
Discharge the patient safely on Warfarin alone  
 Stopping Heparin too early puts the patient at high risk for clot  
formation, since Warfarin hasn't kicked in yet.
