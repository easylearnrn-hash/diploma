❤ 1. ANTIHYPERTENSIVES (LOWER BLOOD  
PRESSURE)  
 ACE Inhibitors  
• Lisinopril, Enalapril, Captopril  
→ (Blocks angiotensin → ↓ blood pressure and ↓ workload on heart)  
→ Side effects: (dry cough, hyperkalemia, hypotension, angioedema)  
 ARBs (Angiotensin II Receptor Blockers)  
• Losartan, Valsartan  
→ (Relaxes blood vessels → ↓ blood pressure, protects kidneys)  
→ Side effects: (hyperkalemia, dizziness, NO dry cough)  
流 Beta-Blockers  
• Metoprolol, Atenolol, Propranolol  
→ (↓ heart rate & force → ↓ blood pressure and oxygen demand)  
→ Side effects: (bradycardia, fatigue, masks hypoglycemia)  
 Calcium Channel Blockers  
• Amlodipine, Nifedipine, Diltiazem, Verapamil  
→ (Relaxes blood vessels and slows heart → lowers BP and chest pain)  
→ Side effects: (edema, constipation, bradycardia)  
 Diuretics  
• Furosemide (loop) → (↓ fluid volume, ↓ BP) → (hypokalemia,  
dehydration, ototoxicity)  
• Hydrochlorothiazide (thiazide) → (mild diuresis) → (hypokalemia,  
photosensitivity)  
• Spironolactone (potassium-sparing) → (keeps K+) → (hyperkalemia,  
gynecomastia)  
ꢀ 2. ANTIANGINALS (TREAT CHEST PAIN)  
 Nitrates  
• Nitroglycerin, Isosorbide  
→ (Dilates vessels → more blood to heart, less workload)  
→ Side effects: (headache, hypotension, dizziness)  
 Beta-Blockers  
• Same as above (also reduce angina by lowering heart’s oxygen needs)  
 Calcium Channel Blockers  
• Same as above (used especially in Prinzmetal angina)  
 3. HEART FAILURE MEDS  
 Digoxin  
• Digoxin  
→ (↑ heart contraction strength, ↓ HR)  
→ Side effects: (toxicity = vision changes, nausea, arrhythmias — check  
pulse!)  
 ACE Inhibitors & ARBs  
• Same as above (reduce workload and protect kidneys)  
 Diuretics  
• Furosemide, Spironolactone  
→ (removes excess fluid → eases breathing, swelling)  
離 Beta-Blockers  
• Used in low doses to reduce long-term stress on heart  
喝 4. ANTICOAGULANTS / ANTIPLATELETS /  
THROMBOLYTICS  
立 Anticoagulants  
• Heparin (IV/SubQ) → (fast-acting clot prevention) → (bleeding risk,  
monitor aPTT)  
• Warfarin → (long-term clot prevention) → (bleeding risk, monitor INR,  
avoid vitamin K foods)  
• Enoxaparin (Lovenox) → (low molecular weight heparin) → (less  
monitoring)  
 Thrombolytics  
• tPA (alteplase)  
→ (breaks up existing clots)  
→ Side effects: (serious bleeding — used in strokes, MI)  
林 Antiplatelets  
• Aspirin, Clopidogrel  
→ (prevents platelets from clumping → prevents heart attack/stroke)  
→ Side effects: (bleeding, GI upset)  
 5. ANTIARRHYTHMICS  
• ⚡ Amiodarone  
→ (Slows abnormal heart rhythms like AFib, VTach)  
→ Side effects: (lung toxicity, thyroid issues, blue skin tint)  
• ⛔ Adenosine  
→ (stops heart briefly to reset it — used for SVT)  
→ Side effects: (brief chest pain, flushing, asystole)  
遼 6. CHOLESTEROL MEDS (STATINS)  
 Statins  
• Atorvastatin, Simvastatin, Rosuvastatin  
→ (↓ LDL, ↑ HDL → prevents plaque build-up)  
→ Side effects: (muscle pain, liver issues, avoid grapefruit)
