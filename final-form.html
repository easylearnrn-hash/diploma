📋 Regulatory Compliance Notice

The information requested in this questionnaire is required to establish an accurate and verifiable personal, educational, and residency timeline for each applicant. This data is essential to ensure institutional compliance with academic regulations, accreditation standards, and external regulatory requirements.

Specifically, this information is collected to:

  • Verify educational history and eligibility for enrollment, credit evaluation, and degree conferral
  • Establish an accurate residency and travel timeline for clinical hour validation, regulatory audits, and licensure pathway assessments
  • Ensure compliance with Armenian Ministry of Education and Health standards, as well as international nursing regulatory requirements
  • Support transcript preparation, certification, and official documentation submitted to licensing authorities
  • Maintain accurate records for academic integrity, institutional accountability, and student protection

Due to the regulatory nature of nursing education and licensure, even minor discrepancies in dates (including single-day differences) may impact eligibility determinations, clinical verification, or external evaluations. Applicants are therefore required to provide information that is complete, precise, and truthful.

All information collected is handled in accordance with institutional privacy policies and is used solely for academic, regulatory, and compliance-related purposes.
