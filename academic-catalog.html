Institutional Overview

The Armenian College of Nursing & Health Sciences is dedicated to preparing competent, ethical, and practice-ready nurses. Instruction is delivered through didactic coursework, skills laboratories, simulation-based education, and supervised clinical experiences.

For licensure-related requirements, eligibility is determined by the applicable nursing regulatory authority.

Programs of Study

The College offers professional nursing programs designed to prepare graduates for entry-level registered nursing practice and eligibility to pursue licensure examinations, subject to regulatory requirements.

Program descriptions, course sequences, and clinical requirements are provided by the College and may be updated to maintain academic quality and regulatory compliance.
