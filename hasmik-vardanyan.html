Hasmik Vardanyan, M.D., is a dedicated healthcare professional and educator whose career reflects long-standing service to medicine, healthcare education, and patient care. With a professional background rooted in clinical practice, functional diagnostics, and medical instruction, she has devoted many years to supporting patients and preparing future healthcare professionals for the responsibilities of clinical practice.

Her professional academic foundation includes graduation from Yerevan State Medical University after Mkhitar Heratsi in 1987, followed by residency and specialized postgraduate training in Functional Diagnostics at the National Institute of Health named after Academician S. Avdalbekyan in Yerevan, completed in 1989. This academic and clinical training strengthened her knowledge of diagnostic medicine, clinical assessment, patient evaluation, and the careful observation required in healthcare practice.

Professional Background

Throughout her career, Ms. Vardanyan has gained valuable experience in medical environments where professionalism, precision, compassion, and responsibility are essential. Her background in functional diagnostics has shaped her ability to approach patient care with accuracy, clinical reasoning, and a strong understanding of disease processes.

Following her graduation from YSMU in 1987, Ms. Vardanyan completed postgraduate residency training in Functional Diagnostics at the National Institute of Health named after Academician S. Avdalbekyan (1989). Building on this clinical and diagnostic foundation, she has dedicated her professional career to healthcare practice and medical education — bringing specialized expertise in immune system disorders and infectious disease processes to her role as an instructor at ACNHS.

Teaching & Clinical Education

Ms. Vardanyan has made a meaningful contribution to healthcare education through her teaching of Autoimmune Diseases and Infectious Diseases. Through her instruction, she helps students understand complex immune-system disorders, infectious disease processes, clinical manifestations, infection control principles, and the importance of safe, evidence-based care. Her teaching reflects experience, clarity, discipline, and a sincere commitment to student development.

Through her work as an educator, she has helped students build the knowledge and confidence necessary to understand challenging medical conditions and apply that understanding in healthcare practice. Her influence extends beyond the classroom, strengthening students' appreciation for clinical reasoning, patient safety, and compassionate care.

Dedication & Values

Hasmik Vardanyan represents the noble values of healthcare education: service, integrity, practical knowledge, discipline, and dedication to others. Her work continues to contribute to the preparation of future healthcare professionals and to the strengthening of medical and nursing education.

Her clinical background has directly enriched her role as an educator — bringing real-world perspective, professional discipline, and patient-centered values into every lesson. Students under her guidance gain not only clinical knowledge but also the professional character and ethical grounding that healthcare practice demands.

Through decades of service in medicine and education, Ms. Vardanyan embodies the commitment to healing, teaching, and continuous growth that defines the faculty of the Armenian College of Nursing & Health Sciences.
