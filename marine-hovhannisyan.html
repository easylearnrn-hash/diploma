Ophthalmic Nursing Nurse Since 1992 Basic Medical School No. 1 S. V. Malayan Ophthalmological Center

Marine Hovhannisyan is an experienced nursing professional and educator whose career reflects more than three decades of dedicated service in ophthalmic nursing, patient care, clinical support, and professional development. Since 1992, she has worked as a nurse in the S. Ohanian Laser Department of the S. V. Malayan Ophthalmological Center, where she has developed extensive expertise in ophthalmic patient care and clinical nursing practice.

Her educational foundation includes studies from 1989 to 1992 at Basic Medical School No. 1, where she trained in the specialty of midwifery. This formal healthcare education, combined with decades of clinical nursing experience, has provided her with a broad practical foundation in patient care and professional healthcare responsibilities.

Professional Background

Throughout her career, Ms. Hovhannisyan has gained substantial experience in preoperative and postoperative care, nursing support for laser procedures, patient education, and accurate medical documentation. Her long-standing work in a specialized ophthalmic environment has required precision, responsibility, patient-centered communication, and close collaboration with physicians and other healthcare professionals.

Ms. Hovhannisyan has served continuously as a nurse at the S. V. Malayan Ophthalmological Center since 1992, providing her with more than thirty years of direct clinical experience. This extensive professional history has given her a mature understanding of safe nursing practice, continuity of care, clinical organization, and the practical responsibilities associated with caring for patients undergoing specialized ophthalmic treatment.

Continuing Education & Professional Development

Ms. Hovhannisyan has demonstrated a strong commitment to continuing professional education and lifelong learning. Her professional development includes specialized training in ophthalmic nursing, courses related to innovative technologies in outpatient care, conferences on oncology, diabetes, healthy lifestyles, nursing quality and safety, and participation in major ophthalmological and healthcare meetings. In 2023, she received 140 continuing professional development credits with authorization for independent professional practice, reflecting her continued engagement with current standards of nursing care.

Her recent professional activity also includes participation in AMWC 2025 in Madrid, Spain, the Armenian International Ophthalmological Conference, the Armenian-French Nursing Conference on Diabetes, and other national and international educational initiatives. These activities demonstrate her sustained commitment to maintaining current clinical knowledge and continuously strengthening her professional practice.

Teaching & Clinical Education

At the Armenian College of Nursing & Health Sciences (ACNHS), Ms. Hovhannisyan serves as an Instructor of Nursing Fundamentals and Clinical Skills, bringing more than three decades of direct nursing experience into the academic environment. Her instruction helps students develop the essential practical competencies required for safe and professional nursing care, including patient assessment, infection prevention, hygiene, documentation, pre- and postoperative care, patient education, and fundamental bedside skills. Through her teaching, she emphasizes precision, professionalism, patient safety, compassionate care, and the disciplined application of nursing principles in real clinical settings.

As an educator, Ms. Hovhannisyan offers students the perspective of a nurse whose knowledge has been shaped by decades of direct patient care in a highly specialized medical environment. Her ability to connect nursing theory with real-world clinical practice helps students build confidence, technical competence, and a strong understanding of professional responsibility.

Fluent in Armenian, Russian, and English, Ms. Hovhannisyan also brings valuable communication skills to both patient care and education.

Dedication & Values

Marine Hovhannisyan represents the values at the heart of the nursing profession: compassion, clinical competence, responsibility, precision, and lifelong learning. Through her many years of service in ophthalmic nursing and her contribution to nursing education at ACNHS, she continues to help prepare future healthcare professionals for safe, ethical, and competent clinical practice.
