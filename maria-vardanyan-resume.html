Professional Profile

Dr. Maria Vardanyan, M.D. is a distinguished ophthalmologist whose professional career reflects longstanding dedication to clinical excellence, continuous advancement, and specialized service in vitreoretinal medicine and surgery. With over fifteen years of experience in ophthalmology, she is recognized for her commitment to patient care, ongoing professional development, and meaningful engagement in both national and international ophthalmic education.

In addition to her clinical practice, Dr. Vardanyan serves as an Instructor of Nursing — Fundamentals & Clinical Skills at the Armenian College of Nursing & Health Sciences, where she brings deep medical knowledge and practical expertise to the preparation of nursing professionals.
