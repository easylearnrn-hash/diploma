Overview

How Grading Works

Your final course grade is determined by four weighted components. Each component reflects a different aspect of your nursing education — theory, practice, clinical skill, and professional responsibility.

The minimum passing grade for all nursing courses is 75%. Grades below 75% constitute course failure and may affect your progression in the program.

Use the Grade Calculator at the bottom of this page to estimate your final grade based on your current scores.

Grade Components

What Makes Up Your Final Grade

Your final grade is calculated using four components, each with a defined weight:

ComponentWeightWhat It Covers
Examinations & Tests40%Written exams, quizzes, proctored tests
Assignments & Assessments15%Homework, care plans, written assignments
Clinical, Skills & Simulation25%Skills lab, simulation, hospital clinical
Attendance & Professionalism20%Attendance record, punctuality, conduct

Examinations (40%)

Examinations test your mastery of course content. They include:

  • Unit exams and quizzes throughout the course
  • Midterm and final examinations
  • NCLEX-style practice assessments

Assignments & Assessments (15%)

This component includes all written and project-based assignments:

  • Nursing care plans
  • Drug calculation worksheets
  • Research-based written assignments
  • Online learning module completions

Clinical, Skills & Simulation (25%)

Clinical performance is evaluated across three settings:

  • Skills Lab: Psychomotor competency check-offs (e.g., IV insertion, wound care)
  • Simulation: High-fidelity scenario performance
  • Hospital Clinical: Evaluated by clinical faculty during rotations

Clinical Safety Override: Failure to demonstrate clinical safety results in course failure, regardless of other component scores.

Attendance & Professionalism (20%)

Attendance is mandatory in all nursing courses. This component evaluates:

  • Presence in didactic classes, skills labs, simulation, and clinical
  • Punctuality and preparedness
  • Professional conduct and communication

More than 2 absences in a single calendar month results in automatic removal (drop) from the course. Attendance violations cannot be appealed.

Calculation Formula

The Official Grade Formula

Final Grade Formula
Final Grade =
(Exams × 0.40) +
(Assignments × 0.15) +
(Clinical × 0.25) +
(Attendance × 0.20)

Example calculation:

  • Exams: 82 × 0.40 = 32.8
  • Assignments: 90 × 0.15 = 13.5
  • Clinical: 88 × 0.25 = 22.0
  • Attendance: 95 × 0.20 = 19.0

Final Grade: 32.8 + 13.5 + 22.0 + 19.0 = 87.3 — Grade B (Satisfactory)

Final grades are rounded to one decimal place. A grade of 74.9% does not round up to 75%.
