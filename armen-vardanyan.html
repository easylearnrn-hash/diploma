Dr. Armen Hrachik Vardanyan, M.D., Ph.D., Doctor of Medical Sciences, Professor, is a distinguished ophthalmologist, scholar, surgeon, and educator whose name is held in the highest regard in Armenian medicine. Widely recognized for his exceptional contributions to vitreoretinal surgery, ophthalmic trauma care, academic medicine, and physician training, Professor Vardanyan represents the highest standards of medical excellence, intellectual leadership, and public service.

With a career spanning decades of clinical, scientific, and academic achievement, Professor Vardanyan has played a central role in the advancement of ophthalmology in Armenia. He has served as Professor at the Department of Eye Diseases of Yerevan State Medical University after Mkhitar Heratsi, Head of the Vitreoretinal Department, Head of the Traumatology Department at the Ophthalmological Center named after S. V. Malayan, and for many years as Chief Ophthalmologist of Yerevan. Through these prominent roles, he has made a profound and lasting impact on both patient care and the education of future generations of physicians.

Professor Vardanyan's academic and professional formation reflects a rare depth of commitment to mastery, service, and innovation. After graduating from Yerevan State Medical University and completing his residency in ophthalmology, he pursued advanced specialization and research in Armenia, Moscow, and the United States. As the first Fellow of the Armenian EyeCare Project, he completed advanced clinical training in retinal and vitreous diseases at the University of California, Irvine, under the mentorship of distinguished leaders in the field. This transformative experience enabled him to bring advanced surgical knowledge, modern ophthalmic techniques, and renewed professional confidence back to Armenia, helping strengthen highly specialized eye care services in the country.

Education & Mentorship

As a university professor and mentor, Dr. Vardanyan is deeply respected for his devotion to medical education and the development of young physicians. His teaching is defined by scholarly discipline, clinical precision, and an enduring commitment to the professional and ethical formation of future doctors. Through his years of instruction and daily guidance of residents, physicians, and trainees, he has shaped the professional lives of many in the field of ophthalmology. His legacy extends far beyond his own clinical practice, living on in the many specialists he has trained, inspired, and guided.

Professor Vardanyan is also admired for his humanitarian spirit and unwavering dedication to helping patients facing the most serious and complex eye conditions. Throughout his career, he has restored not only sight, but also hope, dignity, and quality of life to countless individuals. His professional life has been guided by a profound sense of responsibility to serve, heal, and elevate the standard of care available to the people of Armenia.

Scholarly Contributions

In addition to his outstanding clinical and educational work, Professor Vardanyan has made meaningful contributions to ophthalmic science and research. He is the author of numerous scientific publications and has contributed important academic work in retinal pathology, conjunctival disorders, clinical immunology, and advanced ophthalmic treatment methods.

His scholarly achievements reflect a lifelong dedication to the advancement of medical science and to the strengthening of Armenia's academic and clinical excellence. Professor Vardanyan's research continues to inform both instructional practice and clinical decision-making in ophthalmology across the region.

Recognition & Awards

For his distinguished service and exceptional achievements, Professor Vardanyan has received significant national and professional recognition. These honors reflect not only his remarkable professional accomplishments, but also his enduring contribution to the nation and to Armenian medicine.

  • Mkhitar Heratsi Medal
  • President's Award in the Sphere of Medicine — Republic of Armenia
  • Special Medal from the Ministry of Defense of the Republic of Armenia

Professor Armen Hrachik Vardanyan stands as an eminent representative of Armenian medical scholarship and a model of academic distinction, surgical excellence, and humane service. His life and career embody honor, intellect, compassion, and dedication. Through his leadership, scholarship, and selfless service, he has not only advanced the science of ophthalmology, but has also elevated the prestige of Armenian medicine both nationally and internationally.
