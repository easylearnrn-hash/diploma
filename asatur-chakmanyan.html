Dr. Asatur Chakmanyan, M.D., is a distinguished physician and emerging academic clinician whose professional journey reflects excellence in internal medicine, hematology, transfusion medicine, and medical education. Recognized for his strong academic foundation, international clinical training, and commitment to patient care, Dr. Chakmanyan represents a new generation of physicians dedicated to advancing medicine through clinical service, scholarship, and teaching.

Dr. Chakmanyan is currently pursuing residency training in Internal Medicine in Palm Springs, California, United States, where he is engaged in the care of complex hospitalized patients and participates actively in multidisciplinary clinical management at Desert Regional Medical Center. His ongoing postgraduate training in the United States reflects both his academic achievement and his dedication to professional growth within highly demanding medical environments.

He earned his Doctor of Medicine degree with highest distinction from Yerevan State Medical University after Mkhitar Heratsi in 2020 — an honor awarded in recognition of outstanding academic performance and scholarly excellence. Following graduation, he pursued advanced fellowship training in Transfusion Medicine and Blood Banking at the Yeolyan Hematology and Oncology Center, developing specialized expertise in immunohematology, blood product management, and the clinical application of transfusion medicine in patients with complex hematologic and oncologic needs.

Education & Licensure

Graduated with Highest Distinction — Yerevan State Medical University after Mkhitar Heratsi, 2020. All components of the United States Medical Licensing Examination (USMLE) successfully completed.

Dr. Chakmanyan's broad international experience has enabled him to develop a well-rounded medical perspective that integrates rigorous scientific knowledge, practical clinical judgment, and a deep commitment to evidence-based patient care. His successful completion of the USMLE and entry into a competitive U.S. residency program reflects sustained academic excellence and professional readiness at the highest level.

During the COVID-19 pandemic, Dr. Chakmanyan served as a frontline physician at the National Burn Center of Armenia, where he contributed to the care of critically ill patients during a period of exceptional strain on the healthcare system. He also served as an Emergency Physician at Ejmiatsin Medical Center, where he strengthened his clinical skill in urgent and emergent care settings. These experiences reflect not only his medical capability, but also his resilience, professionalism, and readiness to serve in times of national and public health crisis.

Academic Interests & Scholarly Work

In addition to his clinical responsibilities, Dr. Chakmanyan has demonstrated a sustained dedication to academic medicine and education. He previously served as a Lecturer in Human Physiology and Pharmacology at Mkhitar Gosh Armenian-Russian International University, contributing to the education and professional development of future healthcare professionals. He has remained actively engaged in teaching through mentorship, structured educational initiatives, and the development of comprehensive review programs focused on high-yield clinical concepts. His work as an educator reflects both intellectual discipline and a genuine commitment to fostering the next generation of medical and nursing professionals.

Dr. Chakmanyan's academic interests include internal medicine, hematology and oncology, transfusion medicine, and emerging cellular therapies. He has a growing interest in clinical research and has contributed to scholarly work and publications in hematology, oncology, and internal medicine. His long-term professional vision is centered on advancing academic medicine through the integration of clinical excellence, research, and medical education, while also promoting international collaboration and the exchange of knowledge across healthcare systems.

Dr. Asatur Chakmanyan stands as a promising and highly respected physician whose career is marked by knowledge, discipline, service, and ambition. Through his work in patient care, medical teaching, and academic development, he continues to build a professional legacy grounded in excellence and dedicated to the advancement of modern medicine both nationally and internationally.
