🔥 Burns

NCLEX-RN High-Yield Notes

1. What Is a Burn?

Damage to skin and tissues caused by heat, chemicals, electricity, or radiation.

Severity Depends On

  • Depth — how deep the burn penetrates
  • TBSA — Total Body Surface Area affected
  • Location — face, hands, feet, genitals, airway = more serious

5. Fluid Resuscitation — Parkland Formula

Adults: 4 mL × %TBSA × weight (kg)
Children: 3 mL × %TBSA × weight (kg)

Give ½ in first 8 hours → then ½ over next 16 hours
Use Lactated Ringer's (LR)
  • LR replaces fluids/electrolytes and raises pH
  • Monitor urine output 0.5–1 mL/kg/hr (adults)

6. Airway Burns — Life-Threatening 🚨

INTUBATE EARLY before airway swelling closes off!

Signs of Inhalation Injury

  • Soot around mouth/nose
  • Singed nasal hairs
  • Hoarseness
  • Stridor (high-pitched breathing = airway closing)
  • Confusion or cherry-red skin (carbon monoxide poisoning)

7. Three Phases of Burn Care

A. Emergent Phase (0–48 hrs) — Save the Life

  • Priority: Airway → Fluids → Prevent Shock
  • Intubate if inhalation injury suspected
  • Start Parkland formula (LR)
  • IV opioids for pain (morphine, fentanyl)
  • Cover with clean, dry sheet

B. Acute Phase (48 hrs–weeks) — Heal the Wound

  • Sterile wound care; antibiotic ointments (silver sulfadiazine)
  • Debridement; skin grafts if needed
  • High-calorie, high-protein diet (burns ↑ metabolism)

C. Rehabilitation Phase (months–years) — Restore Function

  • ROM exercises to prevent contractures
  • Compression garments to reduce scarring
  • Psychosocial support (PTSD, body image, anxiety)

8. Special Burns

⚡ Electrical Burns

  • Hidden damage to heart, brain, muscles
  • Monitor cardiac rhythm (risk of V-fib)
  • Check urine for myoglobin → rhabdomyolysis risk
  • High IV fluids to protect kidneys
  • Use Mafenide (Sulfamylon) — penetrates eschar

🧪 Chemical Burns

  • Remove contaminated clothing immediately
  • Flush skin with water for 20–30 minutes
  • Dry powder: brush off first, then rinse

10. NCLEX Must-Know Priorities

  • ✅ Airway first — soot, hoarseness, singed hairs → intubate
  • ✅ Parkland formula — LR, ½ first 8 hrs, ½ next 16 hrs
  • ✅ IV opioids only in emergent phase (IM/PO absorption unreliable)
  • ✅ Sterile wound care — Silver sulfadiazine for 2nd/3rd°
  • ✅ High-protein, high-calorie diet supports healing
  • ✅ Psychosocial — PTSD, body image, long-term support
Why not IM/PO meds early? Burns → poor peripheral perfusion → unreliable absorption. Use IV only.
