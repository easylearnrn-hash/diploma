🩹 Skin Conditions Overview

NCLEX-RN High-Yield Notes

1. What Is a Skin Condition?

Any disorder affecting skin, hair, or nails. May be temporary (rash) or chronic (long-term).

Causes

  • Infections — bacterial, viral, fungal, or parasitic
  • Inflammation — allergic reactions, autoimmune diseases
  • Environmental — sun exposure, irritants, humidity

3. Treatment by Category

A. Bacterial (Impetigo, Cellulitis, Folliculitis)

  • Topical antibiotics (mupirocin, bacitracin) for minor cases
  • Oral antibiotics (cephalexin, clindamycin) for severe cases
  • Keep area clean; avoid touching lesions

B. Viral (Shingles, Herpes Simplex, Warts)

  • Antiviral medications: acyclovir, valacyclovir
  • Pain management: NSAIDs, lidocaine cream
  • Shingles vaccine (Zoster) for adults >50
Shingles precautions: Avoid contact with pregnant women and immunocompromised individuals.

C. Fungal (Ringworm, Athlete's Foot, Candidiasis)

  • Topical antifungals: clotrimazole, miconazole
  • Oral antifungals for widespread: fluconazole, terbinafine
  • Keep skin dry; wear loose, breathable clothing

D. Parasitic (Scabies, Lice)

  • Permethrin cream (kills mites/lice)
  • Wash clothes and bedding in hot water
  • Treat all household members simultaneously

E. Chronic Inflammatory (Eczema, Psoriasis, Vitiligo)

  • Moisturizers to prevent dryness
  • Topical corticosteroids (hydrocortisone, triamcinolone)
  • Phototherapy for vitiligo and psoriasis
  • Sunscreen to prevent worsening of melasma/vitiligo

ABCDE Detection Rule

  • A — Asymmetry
  • B — Border irregularity
  • C — Color variation
  • D — Diameter >6 mm
  • E — Evolving (changing)

5. NCLEX Priority Teaching Points

  • ✅ Avoid scratching — prevents secondary infection
  • ✅ Use hypoallergenic soaps and fragrance-free moisturizers
  • ✅ Fungal infections: keep skin dry, avoid sharing personal items
  • ✅ Bacterial infections: wash hands, cover sores to prevent spread
  • ✅ Shingles: avoid contact with pregnant women and immunocompromised patients
  • ✅ Melanoma: ABCDE rule → any change → urgent dermatology referral
NCLEX Must-Know:
Shingles = acyclovir + pain control
Scabies = permethrin, treat contacts
Ringworm = antifungal cream
Psoriasis = steroids + UV therapy
