🔥🩹 Burns & Dermatology Medications

NCLEX-RN High-Yield Pharmacology Notes

1. Burn Management Medications

💧 IV Fluids — Lactated Ringer's (LR)
Use: Fluid resuscitation in major burns (first 24 hrs, Parkland formula)
Side effects: Fluid overload, electrolyte imbalance
🧴 Silver Sulfadiazine (Silvadene)
Use: Topical antibiotic for 2nd & 3rd-degree burns
Side effects: Painless application, can ↓ WBCs; avoid on face or in pregnancy
Check for sulfa allergy before use!
🦠 Mafenide (Sulfamylon)
Use: Electrical burns or when eschar penetration is needed
Side effects: Painful application, metabolic acidosis (inhibits carbonic anhydrase)
Check for sulfa allergy. Monitor ABGs for acidosis.
💉 Tetanus Vaccine
Use: Given if tetanus status unknown or outdated
Side effects: Fever, soreness at injection site

2. Skin Infection & Wound Care Medications

💊 Mupirocin (Bactroban)
Use: Impetigo, minor skin infections (Staph/Strep)
Side effects: Local irritation
🧼 Bacitracin
Use: Small cuts and abrasions
Side effects: Rash, contact dermatitis
🧴 Topical Corticosteroids (Hydrocortisone, Triamcinolone)
Use: Eczema, dermatitis, allergic rashes — reduces inflammation
Side effects: Skin thinning with prolonged use, irritation
Use lowest effective potency; avoid occlusion over large areas.
🧴 Antihistamines (Diphenhydramine / Benadryl)
Use: Allergic skin reactions, itching (eczema, hives)
Side effects: Drowsiness (oral), dryness

3. Antifungal Medications (Skin)

🍄 Clotrimazole / Miconazole (Topical)
Use: Athlete's foot, ringworm, diaper rash (yeast)
Side effects: Mild irritation or burning
💊 Oral Fluconazole (Diflucan)
Use: Widespread fungal infections, tinea capitis, candidiasis
Side effects: GI upset, hepatotoxicity (monitor LFTs)
🧴 Ketoconazole / Selenium Sulfide Shampoo
Use: Seborrheic dermatitis ("dandruff"), tinea versicolor
Side effects: Scalp irritation, hair discoloration with selenium sulfide

4. Antiparasitic Medications (Skin)

🧴 Permethrin 1% (NIX) — Lice
Use: Head lice — apply to hair/scalp, rinse after 10 min
Side effects: Burning, itching, redness after application
🧴 Permethrin 5% (Elimite) — Scabies
Use: Scabies — apply from neck down, wash off after 8–14 hrs
Side effects: Burning, stinging
Treat all household members simultaneously. Wash all linens in hot water.
