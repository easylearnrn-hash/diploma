Tatevik Anoyan is a distinguished Senior Product Designer whose career reflects exceptional strength in user experience strategy, product innovation, enterprise design systems, and human-centered digital transformation. With over eight years of professional experience spanning healthcare, e-commerce, fintech, beauty, AI platforms, HR management, banking, and marketplaces, she has established herself as a versatile and accomplished design leader known for combining research, storytelling, and strategic execution to create meaningful user experiences.

Throughout her career, Ms. Anoyan has led the design of complex digital products from early concept development to optimized delivery, consistently focusing on user needs, business goals, and measurable outcomes. She is especially recognized for her ability to unite design excellence with strategic impact — designing solutions that are not only functional and effective, but also thoughtful, inclusive, and impactful.
