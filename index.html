Our Distinction

Why Choose ACNHS

ACNHS emphasizes early clinical reasoning, structured skills development, and professional nursing judgment from the beginning of training.

Institutional Affiliation — ANA-US, Inc.

ACNHS maintains a professional collaboration with the Armenian Nurses Association of the United States (ANA-US), Inc., strengthening global nursing integration and providing lifetime professional membership opportunities to every ACNHS graduate.

System-Based Curriculum

Our curriculum is organized around body systems, patient care needs, and clinical application in order to support practical nursing reasoning and structured learning progression.

Clinical-First Training

Students are prepared through a combination of theoretical instruction, simulation, and supervised clinical education. Clinical competence is developed progressively across all terms.

International Perspective

Programs are designed with international academic comparability and documentation clarity in mind, subject to the requirements of external evaluators and regulatory authorities.

Strict Academic Standards

ACNHS enforces clear attendance policies, grading standards, and professional conduct requirements. This discipline reflects real healthcare expectations and ensures graduates meet the ethical and professional standards of a registered nurse.

Clinical Reasoning Framework

Instruction incorporates case-based clinical reasoning, documentation awareness, and applied decision-making relevant to contemporary nursing education.

Digital Transcript & Board Response

ACNHS maintains a modern digital records system. Official transcript requests and verification inquiries from Boards of Nursing or credential evaluation agencies are handled in accordance with institutional procedures, supporting timely documentation handling.

OSCE-Based Competency Assessment

Students complete structured Objective Structured Clinical Examinations (OSCEs) and simulation-based performance evaluations to demonstrate real-world clinical readiness beyond theoretical knowledge.

Academic Programs

Programs of Study

Rigorous, clinically-focused degrees designed for the modern healthcare environment.

Flagship Program · Enrolling

Associate Degree in Nursing

A pre-licensure nursing program focused on foundational nursing science, clinical judgment, and hands-on patient care. The program is designed to prepare students for entry-level nursing practice foundations and for possible pursuit of licensure pathways where permitted by the relevant regulatory authority.

The ADN program provides a strong academic and clinical foundation in nursing science, patient care, pharmacology, and core nursing competencies. ACNHS is a foreign nursing institution, and completion of the program does not by itself guarantee examination eligibility, licensure, registration, or professional recognition in any other country or jurisdiction. Graduates may pursue credential evaluation and regulatory review in the jurisdiction of intended practice, subject to all applicable legal, academic, and professional requirements. All final determinations are made exclusively by the relevant nursing board, council, or licensing authority.

Professional Membership Advantage

Upon graduation, ACNHS students automatically receive lifetime membership in the Armenian Nurses Association of the United States (ANA-US), Inc. — a nonprofit professional organization supporting international nurse integration, professional development, and U.S. career guidance.

Bachelor of Science in Nursing

A four-year program building advanced clinical competence, leadership, research skills, and population health expertise. Designed for students seeking broader academic preparation in nursing practice, leadership, and population health.

The BSN program provides advanced preparation in evidence-based nursing practice, clinical leadership, community and population health, and research-informed care. Graduate Outcomes

What Our Graduates Are Prepared to Do

Upon successful completion of an ACNHS nursing program, graduates will have developed foundational clinical knowledge, professional judgment, and an academic record that may support further steps in their nursing education or practice pathway, subject to the requirements of the relevant jurisdiction.

  • Be eligible to pursue Registered Nurse (RN) licensure, subject to approval by the applicable regulatory authority.
  • Demonstrate clinical readiness for patient care environments, including hospital, clinic, and community health settings.
  • Demonstrate a strong academic and clinical foundation for progression to BSN and advanced nursing studies.
  • Apply clinical judgment, critical thinking, and evidence-based decision-making in complex patient care situations.
  • Receive an official academic transcript issued by ACNHS, available for institutional review and credential evaluation through the institution's verification system.
