Our Distinction

Why Choose ACNHS

We build nurses who think clinically from day one — not graduates who simply passed an exam.

Institutional Affiliation — ANA-US, Inc.

ACNHS maintains a professional collaboration with the Armenian Nurses Association of the United States (ANA-US), Inc., strengthening global nursing integration and providing lifetime professional membership opportunities to every ACNHS graduate.

System-Based Curriculum

Our curriculum is structured by body systems and clinical conditions, not memorization. This mirrors real clinical practice and aligns directly with NCLEX-RN clinical judgment frameworks, preparing students to think like nurses from day one.

Clinical-First Training

Students train in high-fidelity simulation labs, OSCE-style assessments, and supervised hospital placements. Clinical competence is developed early and reinforced continuously — graduates are practice-ready, not just exam-ready.

Global Mobility

Programs are designed to meet international nursing education standards, with transcripts structured for global evaluation. Graduates are prepared for opportunities locally and internationally, subject to regulatory approval.

Strict Academic Standards

ACNHS enforces clear attendance policies, grading standards, and professional conduct requirements. This discipline reflects real healthcare expectations and ensures graduates meet the ethical and professional standards of a registered nurse.

NCLEX-Aligned Clinical Framework

Our curriculum integrates NCLEX-RN clinical judgment models, U.S. documentation standards, and case-based simulation exercises. Instruction is structured around real U.S. clinical reasoning frameworks to prepare graduates for international evaluation processes.

Digital Transcript & Rapid Board Response

ACNHS maintains a modern digital records system. Official transcript requests and verification inquiries from Boards of Nursing or credential evaluation agencies are processed efficiently, ensuring no unnecessary delays in documentation handling.

OSCE-Based Competency Assessment

Students complete structured Objective Structured Clinical Examinations (OSCEs) and simulation-based performance evaluations to demonstrate real-world clinical readiness beyond theoretical knowledge.

Academic Programs

Programs of Study

Rigorous, clinically-focused degrees designed for the modern healthcare environment.

Flagship Program · Enrolling

Associate Degree in Nursing

Accelerated pre-licensure RN program focused on foundational nursing science, clinical judgment, and hands-on patient care. Graduates are prepared for safe entry-level practice and NCLEX-RN eligibility.

The ADN program provides a comprehensive foundation in nursing science, clinical skills, pharmacology, and patient-centered care. ACNHS is a foreign nursing institution, and completion of the program does not by itself guarantee eligibility for U.S. licensure or the NCLEX-RN. Graduates may seek licensure consideration in the United States after diploma and transcript evaluation, satisfaction of applicable regulatory requirements, and formal review by the Board of Nursing or licensing authority in the jurisdiction of application. All licensure decisions are made exclusively by the relevant regulatory authority.

Professional Membership Advantage

Upon graduation, ACNHS students automatically receive lifetime membership in the Armenian Nurses Association of the United States (ANA-US), Inc. — a nonprofit professional organization supporting international nurse integration, professional development, and U.S. career guidance.

Flagship Program · Enrolling

Bachelor of Science in Nursing

A comprehensive four-year program building advanced clinical competence, leadership, research skills, and population health expertise. Designed for students seeking expanded scope and career mobility.

The BSN program provides advanced preparation in evidence-based nursing practice, clinical leadership, community and population health, and research-informed care. ACNHS is a foreign nursing institution, and completion of the program does not by itself guarantee eligibility for U.S. licensure or the NCLEX-RN. Graduates may pursue credential evaluation and licensure review in the jurisdiction of intended practice, subject to all applicable legal, academic, and regulatory requirements. All determinations regarding NCLEX-RN eligibility and licensure are made exclusively by the relevant Board of Nursing or licensing authority.

Professional Membership Advantage

Upon graduation, ACNHS students automatically receive lifetime membership in the Armenian Nurses Association of the United States (ANA-US), Inc. — a nonprofit professional organization supporting international nurse integration, professional development, and U.S. career guidance.

Graduate Outcomes

What Our Graduates Are Prepared to Do

Upon successful completion of an ACNHS nursing program, every graduate exits with the clinical knowledge, professional judgment, and academic record required for licensure and practice.

  • Be eligible to pursue Registered Nurse (RN) licensure, subject to approval by the applicable regulatory authority.
  • Practice in hospital, clinic, and specialty healthcare settings as entry-level registered nurses.
  • Demonstrate a strong academic and clinical foundation for progression to BSN and advanced nursing studies.
  • Apply clinical judgment, critical thinking, and evidence-based decision-making in complex patient care situations.
  • Pursue international career opportunities with a globally recognized transcript and credential portfolio.
