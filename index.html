Why Choose ACNHS

System-Based Curriculum

Our curriculum is structured by body systems and clinical conditions, not memorization. This approach mirrors real clinical practice and aligns directly with NCLEX-RN clinical judgment and decision-making frameworks, preparing students to think like nurses from day one.

Clinical-First Training

Students train in high-fidelity simulation labs, OSCE-style assessments, and supervised hospital placements. Clinical competence is developed early and reinforced continuously, ensuring graduates are practice-ready, not just exam-ready.

Global Mobility

Programs are designed to meet international nursing education standards, with transcripts structured for global evaluation and mobility. Graduates are prepared for opportunities both locally and internationally, subject to regulatory approval.

Strict Academic Standards

ACNHS enforces clear attendance policies, grading standards, and professional conduct requirements. This discipline reflects real healthcare expectations and ensures graduates meet the ethical, academic, and professional standards required of registered nurses.

Programs

Accelerated pre-licensure RN program focused on foundational nursing science, clinical judgment, and hands-on patient care. Graduates are prepared for safe entry-level practice and NCLEX-RN eligibility.

Graduate Outcomes

Upon successful completion of an ACNHS nursing program, graduates are prepared to:

  • Be eligible to pursue Registered Nurse (RN) licensure, subject to approval by the applicable nursing regulatory authority
  • Practice in hospital, clinic, and specialty healthcare settings as entry-level registered nurses
  • Demonstrate a strong academic and clinical foundation for progression to BSN and advanced nursing studies
  • Apply clinical judgment, critical thinking, and evidence-based decision-making, rather than rote memorization

Simulation & Clinical Training

ACNHS provides a comprehensive clinical education experience that integrates high-fidelity simulation laboratories, Objective Structured Clinical Examinations (OSCEs), and supervised clinical practice. Students develop hands-on nursing skills, clinical judgment, and professional confidence through structured simulation scenarios and real-world patient care experiences.

High-Fidelity Simulation Labs

State-of-the-art simulation technology enabling realistic patient care scenarios in a controlled learning environment

OSCE Assessments

Objective Structured Clinical Examinations to evaluate clinical competence and professional decision-making

Supervised Clinical Practice

Hands-on training in approved healthcare facilities with experienced clinical instructors and preceptors

Patient Safety Focus

Evidence-based practice, interdisciplinary collaboration, and professional accountability in all clinical settings

Clinical training is conducted in approved healthcare facilities in Armenia (Yerevan) and through affiliated clinical exposure opportunities in the United States, including Los Angeles, Las Vegas, and New York, providing students with experience across diverse patient populations and care settings and preparing graduates for safe entry-level nursing practice.
