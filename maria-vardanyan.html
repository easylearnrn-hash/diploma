Dr. Maria Vardanyan, M.D., is a distinguished ophthalmologist whose professional career reflects longstanding dedication to clinical excellence, continuous advancement, and specialized service in vitreoretinal medicine and surgery. With many years of experience in the field of ophthalmology, she is recognized for her commitment to patient care, ongoing professional development, and meaningful engagement in both national and international ophthalmic education.

Since 2009, Dr. Vardanyan has served as an Ophthalmologist in the Department of Vitreo-retinal Surgery at the S. V. Malayan Eye Center, one of Armenia's leading ophthalmic institutions. Through her work in this specialized department, she has contributed to the diagnosis, management, and treatment of complex retinal and vitreoretinal conditions, providing skilled care to patients requiring advanced ophthalmic evaluation and intervention.

In addition to her clinical work, Dr. Vardanyan possesses profound knowledge of Nursing Fundamentals and clinical skills instruction, developed through her experience teaching hundreds of students. Her educational work reflects a strong command of foundational healthcare principles and a clear ability to guide learners in the development of essential practical skills.

Education & Training

Dr. Vardanyan received her medical education at Yerevan State Medical University, completing her studies at the Faculty of General Medicine from 2000 to 2006, followed by residency training in ophthalmology from 2006 to 2009 at the Ophthalmologic Center after S. V. Malayan in association with Yerevan State Medical University. Her advanced international training through ESASO and an ESO-grant observership at Hospital San Raffaele in Milan further strengthened her expertise in surgical retina and reflects her commitment to high standards of modern ophthalmic practice.

Scholarly Work & Publications

Dr. Vardanyan has contributed to academic literature through published research in the field of retinal disease and vitreoretinal treatment. Her publication record reflects her engagement with evidence-based medicine and her interest in advancing understanding of complex retinal pathologies and treatment strategies.

  • Retinectomy for retinal detachment complicated by proliferative vitreoretinopathy
  • Intravitreal use of Avastin for treating macular edema caused by thrombosis of the central retinal vein

Dr. Vardanyan has remained actively engaged in the wider ophthalmological community through regular participation in major professional congresses and scientific meetings, including EURETINA, ESCRS, SOE, and AECP congresses, as well as ophthalmology conferences across Armenia and Europe. Fluent in Armenian, Russian, and English, she brings strong communication skills to her professional practice and international engagement.

Dr. Maria Vardanyan stands as a respected physician and educator whose work embodies precision, commitment, and professional integrity. Through her longstanding service in vitreoretinal surgery, her contribution to student education, and her continuous pursuit of advanced knowledge, she continues to make a valuable contribution to specialized eye care and healthcare education in Armenia.
